Volunteering

Volunteering holds a special place in my life. It is not just “helping others”, but an opportunity to be part of something bigger — to take part in solving real problems, to learn teamwork and to understand how the world works beyond the classroom.

My volunteering experience started in September 2023. Since then I have taken part in 38 good deeds and dedicated 216 hours to volunteering (all hours are verified on the dobro.ru platform). The main organizers of the events are KubSU and the “Vysshaya Liga” volunteer league.

Volunteering areas

1. Sport (about 100 hours)

The largest area of my volunteering. I helped organize the biggest runs in Krasnodar: Beauty Run 2026 and Hard Run 2026 (“Vysshaya Liga”), “Magnit Gorod 231” (2024), “Gorod 230” (2023), Za Beg.RF (2024), “Cross of the Nation 2024” and the corporate run “Energy of Life” (2023, 2024). I worked as a competition volunteer at sports events: the FC Krasnodar — Lokomotiv match (2025), the Winline Alma Mater Cup match (2025), KubSU — Titan (2024), the Suvorov Trail, the beach volleyball federation cup, the “Gymnastic Symphony”, “Drift-South Matsuri” and the Gamula Superleague Cup award ceremony. At many events I served as a crowd flow coordinator.

What it gave me: experience of working in real time, quick decision-making, team coordination and mutual support skills.

2. Culture and art (about 40 hours)

I helped at the performances of the “Premiera” theater named after Gatov as a seating coordinator (2025), took part in a meeting with the LARGO art group, the “1000 Smiles” festival, filming a video clip for Volunteer Day (2024, as a performer), KubSU Freshman Days (2023, 2024) and the “Bremen Musicians on Ice” show (2023).

What it gave me: an understanding of the value of cultural heritage, communication skills with very different audiences, and the ability to work in a creative environment.

3. Education and mentoring (about 35 hours)

I participated in the NCPTI Curator School (2025), the “Sirius. Summer: start your project” program (2025), organized the KubSU “Student of the Year” Award (2025), the Council of Rectors of Kuban and Adygea (2024), the “Learning by Serving” launch (2024) and the seminar “What we are called to teach our children” (2023).

What it gave me: the ability to explain complex things in simple language, public speaking skills, mentoring experience.

4. Civic initiatives (about 19 hours)

I worked as a volunteer at the Krasnodar Region Governor elections (2025) and the Presidential elections of the Russian Federation (2024), including work with people with disabilities. I also participated in the Election Organizers Forum (2023).

What it gave me: responsibility, attentiveness, the ability to work with people in demanding situations.

5. Veterans and historical memory (about 15 hours)

I helped at the opening of the All-Russian School Olympiad (2026, seating coordinator), participated in the activities of the “Good Deeds. Kuban” foundation (2024), National Flag Day of the Russian Federation (2024) and National Unity Day (2023).

6. Intellectual assistance (about 8 hours)

I worked as an assistant at the self-employed festival “Dvigai Delo” (2024).

All events were rated 5 out of 5 by the organizers. The volunteer book and hours are verified on the dobro.ru platform.

Volunteer training

I completed 9 courses of the Volunteer School on the edu.dobro.ru platform, including “Basic Volunteer Competencies”, “Event Volunteering: Basic Course”, “First Aid 2.0”, “Conflict Management”, “Youth Policy Ecosystem” and others.

Skills that volunteering helped me develop

  • Communication — the ability to talk to different people.
  • Teamwork — distributing roles, mutual support.
  • Responsibility — if you took it on, see it through.
  • Time management — combining studies, research and volunteering.
  • Empathy — seeing the person behind the task.

Main achievement

2023–2026

216 hours of volunteering · 38 good deeds · 6 areas

Over three years of volunteering I took part in 38 events across six areas: from the Vysshaya Liga sports runs to the Presidential elections. Every hour is confirmed in the volunteer book on the dobro.ru platform, and every event was rated the maximum 5 out of 5 by the organizers. Volunteering taught me that the most valuable things you can give are your time and attention.
